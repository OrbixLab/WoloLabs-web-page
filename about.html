About us

We are not here to follow instructions

For a long time, playing meant picking a side. Either you opened a box and followed rules already written, or you turned on a screen and walked into a closed world. Two ways to play, two different sets of limits.

And in between, something was missing: more freedom, more control, more possibility. WOLO was born in that space. Not as another game or another console, but as a hybrid zone where the physical and the digital cross, interact and shift with what you do.

There is no single way to use it. You can try, mix, get it wrong, build it again. And somewhere in that process something important changes: you are no longer just playing. You are creating.

/ Mission

To build hybrid games and experiences that bring together the world of board games and video games, simplifying the experience, driving innovation and opening new possibilities to play and to create.

/ Vision

To be the company that redefines the way people play, connecting the physical and the digital through innovative products, and becoming a global reference inside a new culture of play and creation.

/ Values

Innovation, professionalism, closeness and simplicity, to build play experiences that are modern, accessible and in constant evolution.
