The hybrid zone

For a long time, playing meant picking a side: a box with rules already written, or a screen with a closed world.

Wolo was born in between. A hybrid zone where the physical and the digital cross, interact and shift with what you do.

There is no set path. You can try, mix, get it wrong, build it again. And there you are no longer just playing: you are creating.
